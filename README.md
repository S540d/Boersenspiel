# Börsenspiel – Barbell Portfolio Dashboard

**📊 Dashboard:** [s540d.github.io/Boersenspiel](https://s540d.github.io/Boersenspiel/)

A virtual portfolio that follows a barbell strategy: a small, low-volatility "safety" part (e.g. a global bond ETF and gold) paired with a larger "growth" part (broad equity ETFs and Bitcoin) — instead of one broad blend. Nothing here is real money.

## What you can see

- **Overview of all strategies and scenarios**, compared over one common period — return, risk, drawdown and difference to a plain S&P 500 buy-and-hold benchmark
- **Detail pages** per strategy with the development of the virtual portfolio over time
- **Prices updated weekly**

## Please note

This is a learning and tracking project, not investment advice. Instruments were chosen with hindsight, so results show how the rules would have played out on those instruments — not what you would have earned. The dashboard's **Premises page** (in the three-dot menu) explains the assumptions and limitations.
